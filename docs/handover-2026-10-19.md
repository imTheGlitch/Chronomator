## What Darren asked for (his words, condensed)

- Track time spent on a property: increments in SECONDS, not minutes; time
  on calls made through Stonehaven, typing, composing documents, reading
  documents; a grand total so users can budget their time, optimise it, or
  discontinue a property that costs more time than its return is worth.
- Travel times to and from (any travel in relation to the property).
- Build it as a separate AmacrA plugin for all current and future apps,
  including Documator — "it needs to be able to track everything for all
  apps and plugins". Name: Chronomator (19 Oct 2026).
